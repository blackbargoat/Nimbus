Privacy

Your audio never leaves your Mac.

Nimbus uses Apple's on-device speech recognition, so the recording stays on your computer. Only the text goes to Claude, straight from your Mac with your own key, when Nimbus writes notes or answers a question. Your classes are saved on your Mac, not on a server. Always check that your teacher is fine with you recording class.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

The app is free. Notes and answers come from Claude, so you paste your own Claude API key into Settings once (get one at console.anthropic.com). You pay Anthropic directly for what you use, and recording and the transcript work even without a key.

Which Macs does it work on?

Any Mac running macOS 26 Tahoe or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel. Nimbus uses the speech model built into macOS 26, and the first time you press Listen your Mac downloads it for your language.

macOS says it can't check Nimbus. What do I do?

Nimbus isn't in the App Store yet. After you drag it to Applications and open it once, go to System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

Does it work for online classes?

Yes, as long as your Mac's microphone can hear the class. Speakers or a quiet room work best.

What AI does it use?

Notes, answers, and live help come from Claude by Anthropic, using your own API key. The key is saved only on your Mac.
