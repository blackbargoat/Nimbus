Opening Nimbus for the first time

1. Drag Nimbus into the Applications folder.
2. Double-click Nimbus. macOS will say it can't check it for malicious software.
   That's because Nimbus isn't from the App Store yet.
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Nimbus.
4. Click Open, then allow the microphone and speech recognition when Nimbus asks.

You only have to do this once.
